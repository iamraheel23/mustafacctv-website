Frequently asked
questions.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll be happy to help.

Can you recommend a system for my property?

Yes. Tell us about the property, the areas you want to monitor and any preferences. We can discuss suitable options and arrange a survey if needed.

Can I view cameras on my phone?

Yes, many modern CCTV systems support remote viewing through a app. Compatibility depends on the equipment, internet connection and configuration.

Do you maintain existing CCTV systems?

We can discuss troubleshooting, maintenance or upgrades. Share the make and model if you know it, along with the issue you're experiencing.

How much does installation cost?

Costs depend on the property, number and type of cameras, cabling and installation requirements. Request a quote for an estimate based on your needs.
